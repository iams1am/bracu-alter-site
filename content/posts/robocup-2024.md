BRACU ALTER reached the **finals** of the **RoboCup Rescue Robot League 2024** held in **Eindhoven, Netherlands** — our debut at the world stage with the **ALTER Rover V1**.

## Highlights
- Finalist among international rescue robotics teams
- Demonstrated full mission autonomy and teleoperation
- First Bangladeshi team to qualify at this level
